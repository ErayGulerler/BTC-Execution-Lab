How to read the result

June 1–7 supplies the initial forecast. June 8–30 supplies six orders per day. Both schedules see the same hour, order side and quantity. Forecast weights update each day using only the preceding seven days.

The model charges 4 bp in fees, 0.5 bp in half-spread and a participation-dependent impact cost. A missed fill stays in the denominator and its cost is marked at the last close. Historical prices and volumes come from one-minute trades. Quotes, queue position and actual fills are unobserved.

Uncertainty is estimated from 2,000 resamples of three-day blocks. A single month gives limited evidence across market regimes.
